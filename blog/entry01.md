### Phaser

On my SEP11 Freedom project I have decided on using Phaser as my tool. This is because it is good at making 2d games and the game that I want to make is a bullet hell game where you have to dodge bullets that are comming at you in a 2d area. I thinkered with Phaser by watching this [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UAdWDHb3zPQ) to learn about the basics of Phaser and followed along in this [file](../tool/phaser-tinkering/game.js). Next I wanted to learn about sprites and animations so I watched this [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ElAmJj8Tfo8) on how to use sprites and how to apply animation to them. I followed along with this while using a sprite I found online in this [file](../tool/phaser-tinkering2/game.js). As I was watching these videos I also used the [Phaser Documentation](https://docs.phaser.io/phaser/getting-started/what-is-phaser)

### EDP (Engineering Design Process)

I am on the first step of the engineering design process, which is defining. First, I have to find out what kind of game I am making and and think of any ideas for the game. The next step is researching where I will be learning how to make sprites and animation for my game by watching videos and reading the phaser website.
